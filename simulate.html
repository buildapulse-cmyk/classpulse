Practice data (simulation)

Fill a school with a 12-week term of made-up school life so you can see every page working: the 153 sample students, daily registers with some absences, class exercises, homework, class tests and a final exam in four subjects, and topics — plus practice teachers and parents to log in as (every practice PIN is 12345). Everything added is marked as practice data and can be removed in one click.

Classes that already have students keep them — the practice scores and registers are made for those students. Empty classes get the sample students.
